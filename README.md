## Project Description

Rachel's Kota Place is a website created for a local kota business. The website provides customers with information about the business, products, prices, contact details and enquiries.

## Website Pages

### Home

The home page welcomes visitors to Rachel's Kota Place and provides a short introduction to the business.

### About Us

The About Us page provides information about the business, its mission and its vision.

### Products

The Products page displays the available food and drinks together with their prices.

### Contact Us

The Contact Us page provides the business phone number, WhatsApp number and location.

### Enquiry

The Enquiry page contains a form where customers can submit their details and enquiries.
