About Rachel's Kota Place

Good food, friendly service, and delicious kotas at Ga-Molepo.

Who We Are

Rachel's Kota Place is a popular local restaurant specializing in delicious, hygienically prepared, and affordable kota meals, crispy chips, milkshakes, and cold drinks.

We aim to give our customers exceptional food, friendly service, and a welcoming dining or takeout experience.

Our Core Values

Quality Ingredients: Freshly sourced polony, cheese, russians, chips, and freshly baked bread for every kota.

Customer Care: Serving every patron with a smile, prompt service, and care.

Affordability: Ensuring great meals remain accessible and budget-friendly for everyone.

Our Offerings

From the classic Princess Kota to the grand Empire Kota, plus sweet milkshakes and ice cream, we cater to all food lovers in the Ga-Molepo community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality, tasty, and affordable food while serving our customers with care and respect in a clean restaurant environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading trusted local restaurant and food brand known for mouthwatering kotas, affordable prices, and excellent customer service.

Ga-Molepo Restaurant Location

Our main restaurant is proudly located in Ga-Molepo, Polokwane, 0732.

We welcome walk-in customers daily for sit-down or takeaway orders, as well as party and event catering inquiries.
